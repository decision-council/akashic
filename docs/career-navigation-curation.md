# Career navigation curation record

This records the bounded first edition for [#140](https://github.com/egohygiene/akashic/issues/140), under [#40](https://github.com/egohygiene/akashic/issues/40). The canonical guide is [Work and Learning — Start Here](../lists/work-and-learning/README.md#start-here).

## Scope and canonical ownership

Extend the existing Work and Learning collection with a short route through career changes, counseling, apprenticeships and trades, union pathways, offers, disability employment support, reentry, and worker help. Add four distinct public resources in the existing Career Research and Public Support and Applications, Interviews, and Job-Search Safety sections. A new collection or resource topic is unnecessary.

The guide is a U.S.-focused first edition. Massachusetts claims and training links retain their narrower scope; international and remote work remain in the existing section. Resources inform questions and discovery. They do not establish a person's employment status, eligibility, legal rights, benefits, or likely earnings.

Record this ownership decision before editing the lists:

### Existing sources supporting the guide

- [My Next Move](https://www.mynextmove.org/) provides interest exploration; [mySkills myFuture](https://www.myskillsmyfuture.org/) starts with a previous occupation. Treat both as prompts for career research. No assessment or search was submitted.
- [Apprenticeship.gov Career Seekers](https://www.apprenticeship.gov/career-seekers) explains registered apprenticeship and directs people to the employer or sponsor, its job finder, and American Job Centers. It supports the distinction between a paid apprenticeship and preparatory training.
- [BLS Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics](https://www.bls.gov/oes/) supplies occupation and geographic wage estimates. Its existing Business entry remains canonical; no wage figures or projections are copied into the guide.
- [DOL's 2026 classification rulemaking FAQ](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/flsa/misclassification/2026rulemaking/faqs) identifies a proposed rule and distinguishes other legal systems. The guide points to current sources and qualified help without restating a classification test or treating a proposal as law.
- [IRS Manage Taxes for Your Gig Work](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/manage-taxes-for-your-gig-work) distinguishes employee and independent-contractor tax handling and discusses records and expenses. No filing threshold, tax estimate, or individual status determination is reproduced.
- [FTC Job Scams](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/job-scams) supports the payment, fake-check, impersonation, and reporting routes already cataloged. Keep any personal evidence private.
- [Berkeley Negotiating Offers](https://career.berkeley.edu/find-opportunities/negotiating-offers/) provides additional compensation-research context. Add only the more directly useful Evaluate Offer page; negotiation flexibility depends on the actual employer and arrangement.

Checking these references does not certify every legacy resource. Unchanged catalog descriptions and metadata retain their prior provenance.

## Access, metadata, and sensitive content

All four additions are public English web pages with no account required to read the linked material. `access: ["free"]` and `account: "none"` describe that access, not a promise of free training, counseling, benefits, or successful placement. Use `license: "unknown"` rather than infer reuse rights from public access.

Use explicit IDs, source roles and authority, U.S. geography, active status, medium volatility, quarterly review, `linkStatus: "ok"`, and `linkChecked: "2026-09-17"`. Flag financial content, disability-related privacy, and youth-related training scope where applicable. Do not set `reviewed` or use link availability as an eligibility determination.

Keep diagnosis, criminal-record details, identity documents, benefit records, employer correspondence, and private application evidence out of public issues and examples. Ask the actual service about required information and secure intake. Avoid claims that a program guarantees employment, an assessment determines suitability, a contract label settles worker status, or a directory establishes current availability.

## Exclusions and follow-up boundaries

- Do not duplicate the existing wage-data, IRS gig-work, contractor-classification, assessment, apprenticeship, accommodation, or worker-rights entries.
- CareerOneStop Salary Finder, Justice-Impacted, and ReEntry Program Finder Help, RSA's state directory, and NABTU's apprenticeship page returned HTTP 403. Omit them as new entries; existing canonical services are not deleted on the basis of a tool-access failure.
- The National Reentry Resource Center homepage also returned HTTP 403. Keep its existing Legal entry and route employment discovery through local workforce counselors; do not label that practitioner hub a verified personal job-placement service.
- A guessed CareerOneStop offer-comparison URL and a Penn offer-guide URL were inaccessible; Berkeley's verified public checklist fills the offer-comparison gap.
- Omit new job boards, commercial coaching funnels, automated applications, remote/nomad expansion, universal salary targets, and personalized legal or tax instructions.
- [#119](https://github.com/egohygiene/akashic/issues/119) owns work-search evidence and application-pipeline templates; [#120](https://github.com/egohygiene/akashic/issues/120) owns exhaustion transitions; [#122](https://github.com/egohygiene/akashic/issues/122) owns hearing preparation. These remain open follow-ups. The guide links shipped tools, RESEA notice routing, and income help without presenting those planned workflows as delivered.
- [#145](https://github.com/egohygiene/akashic/issues/145) owns the broader credential-verification and education-pathways release. This guide asks readers to confirm a training program's cost, award, and requirements but does not create that larger guide.
