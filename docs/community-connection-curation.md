# Community connection: ownership and source notes

Issue [#151](https://github.com/egohygiene/akashic/issues/151) adds a United States first-edition [decision guide](community-connection.md) and a short [Public Services portal guide](../lists/public-services-and-support/README.md#community-volunteering-and-preparedness). The guide routes a reader through finding, verifying, first contact, participation, and reassessment. It does not maintain local member lists or attest to the safety or availability of a particular group. Source pages and redirects were checked on **2026-09-23**; an event or local chapter must be checked again before participation.

The decision guide distinguishes public libraries and services, registered charities, informal mutual aid, peer groups, and clubs with possible paid membership. Those types can overlap at an event; the reader checks who actually organizes the activity. Entry in the catalog is a discovery aid, never an endorsement or a verification of a local organizer.

## Primary-source observations

| Source checked | Observation supporting the route | Limit and metadata treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Meetup Find Events & Groups](https://www.meetup.com/find/) and [Meetup organizer dues help](https://help.meetup.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002877671--Membership-dues-How-do-I-set-member-dues-for-the-groups-I-organize) | Its public discovery page shows groups and events by topic and location; organizers may set dues. | Public browsing can be free while participation can require an account, dues, or event fees. Direct automated access to the locator returned HTTP 403, although the first-party page was retrievable through web inspection: `linkStatus: unknown`, not a false `ok` or an outage claim. Review quarterly. |
| [USA.gov Libraries and Archives](https://www.usa.gov/libraries) | Official route to finding public libraries. | The library's own event calendar and access arrangements control; no separate list of local branches. |
| [Idealist Volunteer Search](https://www.idealist.org/en/volunteer), [VolunteerMatch on Idealist](https://www.idealist.org/volunteermatch), and [Idealist merger FAQ](https://www.idealist.org/en/about/merger-faq) | Idealist has the volunteer search; the former VolunteerMatch address redirects to an Idealist page. | Keep the search and transition page as distinct existing entries. Search listing does not verify a role. |
| [Mutual Aid Hub](https://www.mutualaidhub.org/) | The existing national map is a starting lead. | The first-party front end was reachable but its individual local groups were not verified. No claim of current capacity, formal registration, safety, or coverage. |
| [211](https://www.211.org/) | Existing U.S. local service referral gateway. | Ask about current intake and eligibility for an actual service; a referral is not an approval or response guarantee. |
| [FTC: Giving to Charity](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/giving-charity) and [IRS: Tax Exempt Organization Search](https://apps.irs.gov/app/eos/) | Public ways to check an organization's identity and claims when money or charity status matters. | Registration is not a safety guarantee, and an informal mutual-aid group is not necessarily a charity. Neither source becomes an extra catalog entry for this issue. |

## Scope, freshness, and validation

- The new records have stable IDs `meetup-groups-and-events` and `ymca-find-your-y`, access that includes possible paid participation, quarterly reviews, and privacy/financial sensitivity. `linkChecked` records only mechanical availability; no `reviewed` date is inferred. Recheck sooner after a redirect, fee/access change, or a report of a broken locator.
- This change adds **two** Public Services resources, raising its declared count from **113 to 115**. It keeps the Awesome Abundance child counts and aggregate unchanged and preserves the existing VolunteerMatch ID during its URL migration.
- Excluded: a new national volunteering catalog, local group endorsements or contact lists, organizer outreach, matching or messaging features, emergency assistance claims for mutual aid, and any inference that a peer group supplies clinical care. Links in the guide point to existing canonical sections instead.
- Validate the Markdown and generated portal with `node scripts/validate-collection.mjs`, `node --test`, pinned Awesome Lint on all three changed resource lists, `node scripts/build-site.mjs`, and `node scripts/check-site.mjs`. Inspect generated guide links, IDs, aliases, counts, and site payload sizes. Generated `dist/` is not committed.
