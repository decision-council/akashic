# Everyday Care and Patient Navigation Curation

This is the bounded first edition for [issue #138](https://github.com/egohygiene/akashic/issues/138), within [roadmap #40](https://github.com/egohygiene/akashic/issues/40). The canonical home is [Health and Well-Being](../lists/health-and-well-being/README.md). Ordinary physical-health navigation belongs alongside the existing mental-health and care resources; a separate collection would split the same patient journey.

## Scope and delivery

The U.S.-focused orientation routes readers to primary and preventive care, dental and vision access, hearing professionals, accessible appointments, referrals, ongoing-care coordination, second opinions, records and corrections, and patient-safety escalation. One prepare → visit → records/referral → follow-up checklist is shared by Markdown and the existing portal guide mechanism. The guide distinguishes free public information, provider discovery, actual care, peer support, and commercial services. The collection advisory now separates 911 emergency response from 988 crisis support.

No diagnosis, symptom interpretation, screening schedule, treatment recommendation, provider ranking, guarantee of free care, or individual coverage determination is added. Reproductive and maternal navigation remains [#139](https://github.com/egohygiene/akashic/issues/139); prior-authorization reimbursement remains [#118](https://github.com/egohygiene/akashic/issues/118). Local provider entries belong in Atlas with explicit applicability, not in this national orientation.

## Metadata and exclusions

All eleven additions have explicit unique IDs, resource type, role, authority, free public web access, U.S. geography, English, account-free reading, unknown license, active status, sensitivity tags, and machine link observations. `access: ["free"]` describes the linked information, not eligibility for free treatment, glasses, devices, records, or a consultation. The reporting page's metadata describes its public instructions; submission can require personal information. No `reviewed` value is added.

Medicare coverage is high-volatility with monthly review; the remaining navigation/education pages use medium volatility and quarterly review. Medical sensitivity applies to all; financial, legal, and privacy tags distinguish access costs, record rights, and reporting. Redirects found while researching new entries do not require aliases because neither older URL was already cataloged.

The duplicate audit covered titles, URLs and variants, aliases, and organization-level overlap across the catalog. The general MedlinePlus entry and its drug reference remain separate from these two task-specific pages. An additional ASHA directory was omitted because the NIDCD professional-role guide plus existing assistance routes supplies a bounded hearing pathway. Broad AHRQ questions material overlaps the selected visit checklist. A NIA appointment article encountered an access challenge; an attempted CMS roadmap path was unavailable, so neither is added or marked verified. Existing commercial providers and local programs were not expanded.
