## Canonical home and overlap decision

Recorded before list edits: establish `lists/family-caregiving-and-aging/README.md` as the canonical home for practical family-support navigation. Childcare discovery, everyday parenting support, and kinship/foster/adoption orientation form a distinct user task that is currently scattered across benefits, health, and legal collections. The collection name also provides a stable home for the later aging/caregiver (#142) and end-of-life (#143) editions; neither is included in this release.

## Metadata, privacy, and exclusions

All seven additions are public English web resources readable without an account. `access: ["free"]` and `account: "none"` describe the linked information, not care, adoption, legal services, benefits, or every linked tool. Use `license: "unknown"` rather than infer reuse rights from public access. Record explicit IDs, source roles and authority, U.S. geography, active status, medium volatility, quarterly review, `linkStatus: "ok"`, and `linkChecked: "2026-09-17"`. Sensitive scopes cover youth throughout, plus medical, legal, financial, or privacy concerns where applicable. Do not set `reviewed`.

The former CCR&R `/ccrr-search-form/` link redirected to the selected `/resources/ccrr-search/` URL. It was never cataloged, so no historical alias is needed. AdoptUSKids redirected its `www` homepage to the bare domain; both selected article URLs loaded on that canonical domain.

- ChildCare.gov returned HTTP 403; omit it as a new entry. The independently verified Child Care Aware routes cover the first edition's discovery and assessment needs.
- Several Child Welfare topic/publication routes were inaccessible, and its national foster/adoption directory returned HTTP 404 on follow-up. Omit them as additions; the existing hub remains in place. The public AdoptUSKids orientation pages and their state-information route were readable.
- A Grandfamilies.org request timed out; use Generations United's verified overview. Do not treat a tool-access failure as proof a service is closed.
- Omit commercial lead-generation directories, child profiles, adoption matching, universal parenting rules, diagnosis, individual custody advice, and copied eligibility thresholds.
- Do not add children's record storage or public examples containing names, dates of birth, addresses, schools, health, custody, or case histories. The guide only asks families to keep necessary evidence privately and use a verified intake channel.
- Leave aging, dementia, adult caregiving, and respite expansion to [#142](https://github.com/egohygiene/akashic/issues/142), and end-of-life planning to [#143](https://github.com/egohygiene/akashic/issues/143). Education-course discovery remains outside this edition.
