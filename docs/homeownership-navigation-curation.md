## Edition and ownership decision

Extend [Housing, Home, and Utilities](../lists/housing-home-and-utilities/README.md) with a U.S. ownership edition: preparing to buy, mortgage documents, payment trouble, foreclosure-help routing, repairs, accessibility modifications, and property insurance. These are stages of finding and keeping a home, so a second collection is unnecessary. A compact ownership router and payment/repair checklist join the existing portal guide; the resource sections supply detail.

## Access, sensitivity, and maintenance

Public guides and finders are free to read without an account. That does not mean homes, insurance, assessments, contractors, loans, or downstream services are free. USDA and VA entries additionally carry eligibility-based access and a program-check date. Their descriptions distinguish program information from an award; loan and grant obligations require direct confirmation before work or borrowing.

Each new or migrated record declares its stable ID, resource type, role, authority, access, U.S. geography, English language, web platform, account requirement, license, status, volatility, review cadence, sensitivities, and link evidence. New NAIC entries use nonprofit authority for the regulator association. The PDF uses proprietary licensing based on its copyright notice; other unknown licenses remain unknown. Program/assistance and flood-insurance navigation use high volatility/monthly review; durable document and consumer explanations use medium volatility/quarterly review. No human `reviewed` date is added.

Keep account and policy numbers, notices, identity documents, disability evidence, photos of possessions, and financial details private. The guide provides contact and documentation questions, not legal representation, mortgage suitability, coverage advice, a contractor endorsement, an affordability calculator, or instructions for construction. Ask about accessible formats, interpreters, paper applications, and in-person help through verified providers. Mortgage deadlines, coverage disputes, liens, and foreclosure notices go to qualified legal help in the relevant jurisdiction.

General insurance planning remains #137; aging/disability service depth and disaster recovery remain their own roadmap slices. No lender rankings, current rates, purported nationwide free-repair grants, temporary-pandemic relief, local provider directory, new dependencies, or runtime changes are introduced. Existing resource identities, rental routes, and payload budgets remain release checks.
