# Massachusetts TOP training, one step at a time

Considering retraining while receiving Massachusetts unemployment benefits? The **Training Opportunities Program (TOP), also called Section 30**, may allow approved full-time training while receiving benefits without ordinary work search. **TOP approval does not pay tuition.** Start with one action below before committing money to a program.

Find the official [TOP application and training references](../lists/work-and-learning/README.md#career-research-and-public-support) in Work and Learning. Return to [Financial Relief](../lists/financial-relief-and-assistance/README.md#massachusetts-top-training) for the short orientation. For missed RESEA tasks, held payments, or ordinary weekly claims, use the existing [RESEA recovery guide](massachusetts-resea-recovery.md).

**Sources checked 2026-09-17.** This guide uses the currently linked eight-page DUA application, official guidance, and Section 30(c). See the [source map and refresh rules](financial-relief-curation.md#top-primary-source-map). Your claim, training, funding, and written determinations control; a school listing or this checklist cannot approve any of them.

## One next action

Open the step matching your situation, complete it, and save the result privately. You do not need to select a career or finish the whole application today.

<details>
<summary>1. Check the deadline and whether training addresses a real skill gap</summary>

**Next action: check the TOP application time remaining in your Unemployment Services for Workers account.** The current [application](https://www.mass.gov/doc/training-opportunities-program-top-application/download) says to return the completed packet **no later than the 20th paid week of the claim**; the [overview](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/training-opportunities-program-top) urges applying before that week. Start well ahead of it so the provider can finish its section. Count paid weeks using the claim record, not simply calendar weeks since job loss.

If the deadline is close, unclear, or apparently passed, contact the TOP Unit through the [official application page](https://www.mass.gov/how-to/apply-for-the-training-opportunities-program-top). Ask DUA to confirm the applicable deadline and whether tolling or a waiver could apply. [Section 30(c)](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleXXI/Chapter151a/Section30) provides exceptions; do not assume you are barred or that asking automatically extends time.

Write a short, factual goal: the work you are seeking, the skill or credential missing, why this training would help, and whether you can attend full time. Include an actual definite return-to-work date if there is one. Compare retraining with ordinary job search or a smaller skill upgrade; TOP is not an automatic reason to enroll in a long course.

Keep meeting current claim, work-search, and assigned RESEA requirements unless DUA has confirmed a change. Applying, paying a deposit, or beginning classes alone does not grant a work-search waiver.

</details>

<details>
<summary>2. Ask the provider to verify the exact offering</summary>

**Next action: send the current application to the person authorized to complete the provider section.** Confirm that they can complete and sign Part C or D, supply the required attachments, and return it before your submission deadline. Use the [program-screening checklist](#screen-a-specific-program), including the online and credit-based distinctions below.

Find candidates through the existing [MassHire JobQuest and training-search entries](../lists/work-and-learning/README.md#career-research-and-public-support). In the public training search, distinguish **Section 30** course approval from **ITA** funding approval and check the approved funding region. A listing is a lead to verify for the exact program, cohort, schedule, and location; it is not your TOP determination or a funding award.

Ask the provider to identify who will verify supervised hours, attendance, curriculum, licensing/accreditation, and placement information. If staff are unsure how a field applies, ask TOP for instructions instead of inventing an identifier or leaving an unresolved field blank.

</details>

<details>
<summary>3. Confirm a funding plan before committing money</summary>

**Next action: ask a MassHire counselor and the provider's financial-aid office what funding you can actually receive.** Use the [funding branches](#funding-is-a-separate-decision) and [comparison worksheet](#program-comparison-worksheet). Mark potential aid as pending until its award, conditions, timing, and payment route are confirmed.

Budget tuition, fees, books, equipment, examinations, transport, and essential living costs. Include payment due dates, withdrawal/refund terms, and the gap if benefits or aid end before training. Ask whether a deposit is refundable if TOP or funding is denied; do not assume either decision will arrive before the payment deadline.

TOP may permit continued unemployment benefits and potentially up to **26 additional weeks of Retraining Extended Duration (RED) benefits** when the applicable conditions are met. This is a maximum, not an award or a promise to fund the whole course. Section 30(c) addresses exhaustion of other benefit rights; DUA determines availability. The application says TOP/RED benefits stop when training ends or available benefits are exhausted, whichever comes first.

</details>

<details>
<summary>4. Assemble the claimant and provider paperwork</summary>

**Next action: complete your Parts A and B, then obtain the completed provider section.** Part B authorizes release of specified education records for the TOP eligibility determination; read it and sign through the official process. You complete the claimant information; the provider supplies and certifies its own information.

Check the packet before submitting:

- Claimant Parts A/B are complete and signed as required.
- The correct Part C or D is complete, signed, and dated by an authorized representative.
- Program dates, scheduled breaks, supervised hours, days, and any term-by-term credits agree with the actual offering.
- Part C includes the official curriculum attachment. For Part D, include any required explanation of a different full-time standard or part-time status; ask TOP whether additional curriculum or supporting records are needed.
- Requested provider identifiers, licensing/accreditation, placement information, and practical-training details are answered accurately. Ask TOP how to document an inapplicable or unavailable field.
- You have a readable private copy of the entire packet and attachments.

DUA says incomplete applications are returned for more information. Provider familiarity with TOP can help administration, but does not replace complete paperwork or an agency decision.

</details>

<details>
<summary>5. Submit and retain proof</summary>

**Next action: submit the complete packet using a current supported DUA route before your confirmed deadline.** The [application instructions](https://www.mass.gov/how-to/apply-for-the-training-opportunities-program-top) currently offer upload through Unemployment Services for Workers or mail to the address shown there. TOP can provide a paper application by phone. Use the live instructions for the address/contact route rather than an old saved form or search snippet.

Save the application version, submitted copy, attachments, date, and upload or mailing/delivery evidence. Confirm what constitutes timely submission for the chosen route; do not assume a postmark is enough. Record a follow-up date and monitor your account and mail for information requests.

A submission receipt is not approval. The current application page says to contact TOP if there is no update after four weeks; contact it sooner if a filing, school-payment, or response deadline requires action. Four weeks is a follow-up instruction, not a guaranteed processing time or an extension.

</details>

<details>
<summary>6. Read the determination and resolve missing information</summary>

**Next action: read the written TOP determination and any request for information.** Verify the approved program, dates, work-search treatment, benefit conditions, and attendance/reporting instructions. Resolve mismatches before relying on a different schedule or course.

If pending, respond through the specified route by the actual deadline and retain proof. Keep filing truthful weekly claims and follow existing requirements until DUA confirms otherwise. If denied or you disagree, protect the notice's appeal deadline and use the [existing DUA appeal route](../lists/financial-relief-and-assistance/README.md#massachusetts-resea-resources); a provider correction or telephone inquiry does not itself file an appeal.

Recheck tuition funding separately. An approved TOP application does not establish a WIOA award, financial-aid eligibility, paid tuition, or reimbursement of an earlier purchase.

</details>

<details>
<summary>7. Follow attendance and reporting requirements during training</summary>

**Next action: record the next weekly claim and attendance-report deadlines from your approval instructions.** These are separate tasks. The current application requires meeting program requirements, attending sessions/classes, completing weekly attendance reports, and taking the TOP survey at completion.

For a missed session, the current application instructs the claimant to call TOP with the actual reason **by 4 p.m. Friday of the week of the absence**. It also assigns absence-reporting duties to providers; do not assume the school's report replaces your call. Use the [current application/contact route](https://www.mass.gov/how-to/apply-for-the-training-opportunities-program-top), verify the reporting procedure, and save the contact record.

Tell TOP about schedule changes, unscheduled breaks, or inability to finish; report work and earnings truthfully on weekly claims. Confirm how changes, practical training, breaks, graduation, withdrawal, or exhausted benefits affect the claim before assuming payment continues. Do not backdate attendance, certify a class you missed, or count homework as supervised instruction.

Keep attendance submissions, change reports, agency responses, and benefit determinations privately. If a problem arises, contact TOP for training approval/rules, the provider for its records, and DUA for claim/payment or appeal questions. Later completion does not guarantee payment for an earlier disputed week.

</details>

## Three separate decisions

| Decision | Who confirms it | Evidence to keep privately |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Benefit eligibility and TOP/RED treatment | DUA/TOP, using the actual claim and application. | Written determination, approved dates/conditions, and reporting instructions. |
| Specific program fit and required paperwork | Provider supplies facts; DUA decides whether the training qualifies. | Completed provider section, curriculum/schedule, supporting explanations, and DUA response. |
| Tuition and other funding | MassHire/WIOA, financial-aid office, scholarship administrator, or other funder. | Award, covered costs, conditions, payment dates, and remaining balance. |

TOP's [official overview](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/training-opportunities-program-top) explains continued benefits and training obligations while explicitly separating training costs. None of the three decisions substitutes for the others.

## Screen a specific program

Ask about the actual cohort you would attend, not just the school's brand or an advertisement. This is a verification checklist, not a score that predicts approval.

| Check | What to obtain from the provider |
| --- | --- |
| Career purpose | Target roles, skills taught beyond what you already know, entrance requirements, and a realistic completion path. |
| Exact offering | Provider and program names, start/end dates, scheduled breaks, days per week, and location/online format. |
| Supervised instruction | Hours per week excluding homework; who supervises, when instruction happens, and how attendance is recorded. |
| Credit path | Credits by term, semester/quarter equivalence, credits already earned and remaining, and full-time status throughout. |
| Paperwork readiness | Named authorized contact, Part C or D, completion date, required attachments, and ability to answer every field. |
| Provider evidence | Licensing/certification or accreditation, relevant PRO/MOSES identifiers, and verifiable placement data for the field of training. Keep completed identifier fields private. |
| Curriculum and practical work | Official curriculum; required apprenticeship, internship, externship, or practicum schedule and supervision where applicable. |
| Outcomes | Credential, portfolio/capstone, placement assistance, and how any placement-rate claim was calculated, including cohort/date and job relevance. |
| Full cost and exit terms | Tuition, mandatory fees, materials/equipment/exams, deposit schedule, cancellation/refund policy, and what happens if approval or funding is denied. |
| Sustainable workload | Class time plus homework, commute, practical work, care responsibilities, accessibility needs, and recovery time. Homework matters to workload even when excluded from supervised hours. |

### Which provider section applies?

These requirements come from the **currently linked application**, especially pages 1 and 4–7. Ask TOP about applicability and exceptions rather than treating a checklist result as approval.

| Path | Current form's instructions |
| --- | --- |
| Part C — vocational, apprenticeship, or other hours-based training | Provider completes Part C. It describes at least 20 weekly hours of supervised in-class instruction, generally completed within two years; it allows three years for the specified apprenticeship/basic-skills situations. The schedule field excludes homework, and the official curriculum must be attached. |
| Part D — college/university or other credit-based training | Provider completes Part D. It describes at least 20 weekly hours of supervised in-class instruction and 12 semester credits per period or equivalent. If the college considers fewer hours/credits full time, obtain its written explanation for DUA. Provide term schedules and requested status explanations; these are not automatic exceptions. The stated duration is two years, with the basic-skills exception. |

The form includes **Online** and **Online Only** choices for both paths. That does not settle how a particular remote or self-paced offering meets supervised-instruction and full-time requirements. Ask who supervises instruction, how hours are measured without homework, how participation/attendance is verified, and whether the provider can certify the actual schedule. Take unclear asynchronous, lab, project, or practical hours to TOP **before relying on eligibility or paying tuition**. Do not declare all online courses eligible or all self-paced courses ineligible.

## Funding is a separate decision

| Possible route | Next verification |
| --- | --- |
| MassHire / WIOA / individual training account (ITA) | Ask a counselor about your eligibility, the specific course and approved funding region, available funds/caps, covered costs, and authorization required before enrollment or payment. A directory listing is not an individual award. Use existing [MassHire entries](../lists/work-and-learning/README.md#career-research-and-public-support) and the [WIOA training finder](../lists/awesome-abundance/public-programs-and-services/README.md#education-training-and-employment). |
| College grants and scholarships | Ask the aid office about the actual credential/program, prior degrees, enrollment load, residency rules, aid application, deadlines, and award timing. Use the existing [Federal Student Aid entry](../lists/awesome-abundance/public-programs-and-services/README.md#education-training-and-employment). |
| Massachusetts community-college or workforce programs | Start with the existing [Massachusetts Free Community College entry](../lists/awesome-abundance/public-programs-and-services/README.md#massachusetts). Current state guidance excludes applicants with a prior bachelor's degree or equivalent and has other conditions. Do not assume experienced degree holders qualify, or that a funding program's part-time standard meets TOP's full-time requirement. Ask about other workforce grants separately. |
| Employer or vendor scholarship | Verify the offer directly with its administrator: eligible course, deadline, amount, service/repayment obligations, and award confirmation. Employer reimbursement after completion may still require upfront money. |
| Out-of-pocket fallback | Compare the confirmed remaining cost with living expenses and a realistic benefit end date. Treat possible RED benefits, aid, and future employment as unconfirmed until supported. Borrowing has repayment terms; a loan is not a grant. |

TOP's benefit decision does not promise tuition reimbursement or make a deposit refundable. Keep a funding denial or delay visible in the comparison rather than covering the gap with an assumed award.

### Experienced workers do not have to start over

Use current job postings and an honest skills inventory to identify the smallest useful gap. These are **evaluation lanes**, not claims of TOP eligibility, placement, or earnings:

| Possible lane | Evidence to seek in a program |
| --- | --- |
| Applied AI / LLM systems | Work beyond introductory prompting: evaluation, integration, data handling, deployment, and a demonstrable project. |
| Cloud / platform / DevEx / DevOps | Relevant production tooling, reliability/security practices, deployment work, and operational tradeoffs. |
| Systems architecture | Design exercises, integration constraints, explicit tradeoffs, and reviewable architecture work. |
| Technical product / program management | Discovery, prioritization, stakeholder coordination, delivery evidence, and realistic transition requirements. |
| Human-AI / creative technology | Interaction or creative-system prototypes, technical depth, user evaluation, and portfolio relevance. |

Compare the course with existing experience, recent openings, and employer requirements. A certificate alone does not prove a career transition or justify debt. If a short portfolio project or targeted course addresses the gap, compare that route too while asking DUA how study affects current claim obligations. Keep the decision about work and capacity separate from choosing a permanent professional identity.

## Keep a private evidence trail

Retain the current application, claim-specific deadline confirmation, provider responses, curriculum and schedules, completed forms/consent, funding applications and awards, receipts, submission/delivery proof, determinations, attendance reports, and change/appeal records. Record the actual date, contact, answer, unresolved item, and next action.

This public guide contains no individual application. Do not publish claimant/student IDs, completed school identifier fields, claim notices, private correspondence, income or benefit amounts, addresses, health details, or screenshots. Share only requested information through verified agency, provider, or funder channels. Refresh the source checks when the state changes the application or Section 30 guidance, and before relying on the rules or publishing derivatives.
