# Massachusetts unemployment is ending: a transition checklist

If your Massachusetts unemployment balance is running low, **first confirm the last payable week, then start protecting essentials before the last deposit**. You do not need to finish this whole guide today. Choose the earliest deadline or the need that cannot wait.

**Massachusetts only · public information and private planning · sources checked September 18, 2026.** DUA determines unemployment eligibility; other agencies decide their own assistance. The extension snapshot below is dated and must be rechecked when used. Return to [Financial Relief and Assistance](../lists/financial-relief-and-assistance/README.md#start-here) for the broader support map.

## 1. Confirm what is ending

An empty bank account, a missing payment, and an exhausted claim are different problems. Read the actual determination, current balance, benefit-year dates, and weekly payment history together. [DUA's claim-status guide](https://www.mass.gov/how-to/check-your-unemployment-claim-status) explains that even **“No Benefit” can mean ineligibility or benefits used up**; that label alone does not settle the reason.

| What you find | Next action |
| --- | --- |
| Regular benefit credit is used up | Ask DUA to confirm exhaustion and the last payable week; check the current options below and plan the income gap. |
| The benefit year is ending | Check the end date separately from the balance. Ask DUA whether a new claim is appropriate and what work/wages and other requirements apply; do not assume another award or a rollover. |
| A hold, eligibility denial/disqualification, information request, or RESEA problem | Follow the notice and its deadline. Use [RESEA and payment recovery](massachusetts-resea-recovery.md); a pending or disputed week is not proof of exhaustion. |
| A weekly claim was missed or the claim is closed | Check the missing weeks and reopening instructions. DUA says a claim can close after three consecutive weeks without a request; reopening does not itself add benefit credit. |
| A payment was issued but has not arrived, or was returned | Follow the payment-status instructions and verify the payment method with DUA. Separate the payable week from the deposit date. |

[DUA's benefit calculation guide](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/how-unemployment-insurance-benefits-are-determined) distinguishes the monetary award from the one-year benefit year. Use your **Notice of Monetary Determination and current claim record**, rather than a generic number of weeks or a count of bank deposits.

In a private note, record:

- Benefit-year start and end dates; regular weekly benefit amount; original benefit credit; current remaining balance; when you checked them.
- Weeks already paid, partial-payment weeks, and any pending, denied, adjusted, or appealed weeks. Keep gross benefits separate from withholding, other deductions, and the net deposit.
- DUA-confirmed remaining payable weeks, possible final partial week, expected last payable week, and separately the expected payment date. Mark an estimate as an estimate.
- Any unresolved eligibility issue, next certification date, actual notice deadline, and contact needed.

Dividing the remaining balance by the weekly benefit amount gives only a rough equivalent of full-payment weeks. Earnings, adjustments, eligibility, and the benefit-year end can change the result. Ask DUA to reconcile differences before treating that arithmetic as a payment schedule.

**A short question for DUA:** “Please confirm whether my issue is exhausted regular benefits, the end of my benefit year, or an unpaid/disputed week. What balance and payable weeks remain, what is my last payable week, and what should I do next about weekly claims or any other benefit rights?” Use the contact routes on the [official workers hub](https://www.mass.gov/unemployment-insurance-for-workers).

### Weekly claims and notices around exhaustion

While still claiming, follow the [weekly-claim instructions](https://www.mass.gov/how-to/file-your-weekly-unemployment-claim), including truthful earnings, availability, and work-search reporting. Do not stop just because the balance is low or an application is pending. Keep the [weekly work-search evidence](work-search-evidence.md) and follow any individual instructions.

**Once the balance reaches zero, ask DUA what continued requests are required for your specific situation**, including disputed weeks or an approved training program. The public instructions reviewed do not establish one universal post-exhaustion certification procedure. If the request option disappears, contact DUA and save the answer; do not invent a workaround, assume a new claim is appropriate, or treat another certification as creating more entitlement.

Check your account's notices, requests, and payment history and the mail you actually receive. Save each notice with its issue date, affected weeks, required action, and deadline. This guide does not promise an exhaustion letter, exact portal wording, or advance-warning interval. If a determination is wrong, use its review/appeal instructions; a phone inquiry does not file an appeal or extend its deadline.

## 2. Check current options, not old extension headlines

**Snapshot checked September 18, 2026:** DUA's [current worker FAQ](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/faqs-about-unemployment-insurance-for-workers) says no benefit extension programs are currently available and directs people whose benefits are ending to Mass 211. Read that general answer alongside the separate, conditional TOP/RED pathway and any claim-specific rights below.

| Route | What is verified and what to do |
| --- | --- |
| Federal-state Extended Benefits (EB) | **Massachusetts is off** in DOL's [Trigger Notice 2026-35, effective September 13, 2026](https://oui.doleta.gov/unemploy/trigger/2026/trig_091326.html). Recheck the [latest trigger notice](https://oui.doleta.gov/unemploy/triggerRedirect.php) and DUA before relying on this snapshot. Even when a state triggers on, [DOL explains](https://oui.doleta.gov/unemploy/extenben.asp) that individual eligibility still matters. |
| TOP / Section 30 / Retraining Extended Duration (RED) | A separate training pathway may provide up to 26 additional weeks when approved and eligible. It is not an automatic extension for everyone whose balance reaches zero. Check the timing branch below immediately. |
| Other claim-specific rights | Ask DUA whether another entitlement applies to the actual facts. For example, [Section 30(d)](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleXXI/Chapter151a/Section30) addresses qualifying employer-lockout cases; it is not general income-gap assistance. Do not count a possible route as an award. |
| Old pandemic-era PEUC, PUA, or FPUC pages | Treat these as historical program information, not evidence that extra benefits are available now. Start from DUA's current FAQ and current federal/state notices, not a saved pandemic article. |

**If no additional UI is confirmed, build the essential-expense plan now.** A proposed law, old program page, application receipt, or adviser suggestion is not spendable income.

### TOP/RED when the deadline is close or has passed

The current [TOP application (PDF)](https://www.mass.gov/doc/training-opportunities-program-top-application/download) says to return the completed application **no later than the 20th paid week**; the [TOP overview](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/training-opportunities-program-top) urges applying before that week. Two weeks before exhaustion may already be too late for the ordinary deadline. Verify paid weeks and the applicable deadline now, rather than counting calendar weeks since job loss.

1. **Before the deadline:** use the [TOP training workflow](massachusetts-top-training.md) to verify the specific program, provider paperwork, submission route, and tuition funding. Allow time for the provider's section.
2. **Deadline unclear, apparently missed, or benefits already exhausted:** contact TOP promptly through the official overview/application route. Ask for the paid-week count, whether tolling or a good-cause waiver applies under [Section 30(c)](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleXXI/Chapter151a/Section30), what evidence is needed, and how to submit for a determination. Record the answer. There are statutory exceptions; neither late eligibility nor an extension of time is guaranteed.
3. **A denial or information request arrives:** read the written decision and act by its deadline. Ask how to correct missing material or appeal as applicable. Keep the actual submission and receipt. Asking a question does not substitute for an appeal.
4. **Approval arrives:** verify the approved training dates, remaining benefit rights, weekly claims, and attendance reporting. The application limits TOP/RED payments to when training ends or available benefits run out, whichever comes first. Until DUA approves a change, keep following existing work-search and eligibility instructions.

**TOP approval, RED eligibility/payment, and tuition funding are separate decisions.** The maximum extra duration is not a promise to cover the whole course, and TOP itself does not pay tuition. Do not commit an unaffordable deposit on the assumption that pending benefits or school aid will arrive.

## 3. Protect essentials before the final payment

Use the routes that fit your household; do not wait for every UI question to be settled before asking about food, shelter, or treatment access. Tell each agency the actual income change, last payable week, and payment dates. Ask what proof and reporting timing it requires rather than assuming DUA updates every other program.

| Need | First route and next question |
| --- | --- |
| Food | Use [Massachusetts SNAP](../lists/financial-relief-and-assistance/README.md#massachusetts-snap) and the [application follow-through guide](massachusetts-snap-follow-through.md). Ask DTA about the income change, required proofs, and expedited screening if appropriate. For food before a decision, use [pantries and emergency food](../lists/financial-relief-and-assistance/README.md#food-and-nutrition). |
| Cash assistance where eligible | Ask DTA about the appropriate cash program, including [TAFDC](https://www.mass.gov/how-to/transitional-aid-to-families-with-dependent-children-tafdc) or Emergency Aid to the Elderly, Disabled and Children (EAEDC). Use the existing [DTA contact and policy routes](../lists/public-services-and-support/README.md#benefits-and-emergency-assistance). UI exhaustion alone does not establish eligibility; ask for the current requirements and application route. |
| Housing and utilities | Start with [rent/housing](../lists/financial-relief-and-assistance/README.md#housing-and-rent), [utilities](../lists/financial-relief-and-assistance/README.md#utilities-and-communications), and [Mass 211](https://mass211.org/) for local intake. Ask who serves your address, whether applications/funding are available, and whether any written protection applies while help is pending. Seek [legal help](../lists/financial-relief-and-assistance/README.md#taxes-and-legal-aid) promptly for a notice with a deadline. |
| Health coverage and medication | Follow the [Massachusetts coverage transition guide](massachusetts-health-coverage-transition.md) for income updates and actual coverage dates. Check [prescription assistance](../lists/financial-relief-and-assistance/README.md#prescriptions) and contact the pharmacy/prescriber about access before supplies run low. A [reimbursement request](prescription-reimbursement.md) remains pending until decided; do not budget it as cash on hand. |
| Disability-related income | Use the existing [SSI/SSDI routes](../lists/financial-relief-and-assistance/README.md#income-and-employment) when relevant. Eligibility and timing are separate from UI exhaustion. Report facts consistently and ask the agencies or qualified benefits help about overlapping work/availability rules; do not treat a new application as immediate replacement income. |
| Local or immediate help | Ask Mass 211 or the [local-assistance routes](../lists/financial-relief-and-assistance/README.md#local-and-disaster-assistance) for the specific unmet need and service area. A referral, waitlist, or closed intake is not an award. |
| Job search or temporary income | Use [Work and Learning](../lists/work-and-learning/README.md#start-here) and the [work-search tracker](work-search-evidence.md). Verify start date, first payday, net pay, and transport/childcare costs. Keep applications and offers separate from pay received; report work and earnings under each applicable program's rules. |

### Make a short bill plan

List what is due before the next **confirmed** income date. Prioritize by immediate consequences for housing, food, utilities, treatment, and ability to work. Include notices and legal dates even when an application is pending.

For each bill, choose one action: pay an affordable amount under the actual terms; request hardship relief; negotiate a plan; or ask about a deferral. Use the [payment-hardship](../lists/financial-relief-and-assistance/README.md#debt-and-payment-hardship), [student-loan](../lists/financial-relief-and-assistance/README.md#student-loans), and relevant provider routes. Ask:

- What is due now, what changes, and when do normal payments resume?
- Will fees or interest continue, is there a later lump sum, and what is the total cost?
- What happens to credit reporting, collections, service, or an existing notice while the request is pending and after approval?
- What evidence is needed, when will you decide, and can you send the terms in writing?

An unanswered request does not pause a bill, shutoff, eviction, or court deadline. Record the provider's actual answer; do not assume a partial payment prevents consequences. A deferral changes timing and is not new income.

## 4. Track support without spending pending money

Use these labels in a **private** worksheet. They organize decisions; they are not agency status codes.

| State | Meaning in this plan | Budget treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **CONFIRMED** | Written award/terms establish the amount or service, conditions, and timing. | Separate future scheduled help from funds actually received and available today. Recheck conditions and missing payments. |
| **PENDING** | Applied, submitted documents, or requested a decision; no final answer yet. | Count zero available cash from this request; keep the next follow-up and a fallback. |
| **POSSIBLE** | A lead or potential program, not yet verified/applied for. | Count zero; identify eligibility/intake and the first action. |
| **NOT ELIGIBLE** | The agency/program has said this route does not apply under the recorded facts. | Count zero; keep the decision/reason and any review deadline. If appealed, track the appeal separately as pending. |

Use three small tables rather than mixing everything into one total. Blank fields mean unknown, not zero or approved.

**Support record — one row per program/request**

| Program or provider | State and evidence date | Help type: cash / restricted benefit / provider payment / service / deferral | Amount or scope; conditions | Expected date; actually received/usable date | Next action, deadline, and private evidence reference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Enter privately] | [Choose a state; notice/receipt] | [Choose type] | [Verified or unknown] | [Keep the two dates separate] | [Action / date / record] |

**Essential bills — one row per obligation**

| Expense | Due date and consequence | Amount still owed | Action and contact date | Written terms / decision / next follow-up |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Enter privately] | [Include notice deadline] | [Verify] | [Pay / request / negotiate / defer] | [Evidence reference] |

**Cash calendar — one row per date**

| Date | Cash actually available at start | Confirmed cash received that day | Cash payments that day | Cash remaining | Future confirmed income and fallback if delayed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Enter privately] | [Available funds only] | [Actual net amount] | [Actual payments] | [Start + received − paid] | [Separate from today's balance] |

Keep a future forecast separate from the actual cash calendar. SNAP is restricted food support; a payment sent directly to a landlord reduces the corresponding obligation once applied; neither becomes unrestricted cash. Do not count the same help both as cash income and as a reduction in the bill. A hardship deferral may leave a later balance even when today's required payment falls.

## 5. Use the transition windows

### About two weeks before the expected last payable week

This is a **planning window, not an agency deadline**. Start earlier when possible; if the last payment already happened, begin with the most urgent step now. TOP and notice deadlines may come much earlier.

- [ ] Confirm the balance, benefit-year end, outstanding weeks, last payable week, and expected payment date with the record/DUA.
- [ ] Recheck the current DUA FAQ and EB trigger; ask about TOP/RED or other claim-specific rights without assuming approval.
- [ ] Keep weekly claims and work-search records current under the applicable instructions.
- [ ] Start the assistance routes that fit; complete missing interviews/proofs and report the income change as instructed.
- [ ] List essential bills, consequences, and current notices; request hardship terms before the due date when possible.
- [ ] Assign each support item one of the four states and a next action. Make a fallback for pending help.

### Final payable week and after the last payment

- [ ] Reconcile the final payable week, gross benefit, deductions, actual deposit, and remaining balance. Investigate a missing payment instead of silently calling it exhaustion.
- [ ] Confirm DUA's instructions for further weekly requests, disputed weeks, or approved training; save the answer and any next deadline.
- [ ] Read new determinations and mail; complete timely responses or appeals through the stated channel.
- [ ] Give other programs the income-change proof they request; verify actual coverage, award, and first-payment dates.
- [ ] Follow up on pending assistance and hardship requests; check that approved provider payments or account changes actually posted.
- [ ] Rework the next week of essential expenses using cash available now. If an essential need cannot wait, contact the relevant provider, local intake, or legal help that day.
- [ ] Continue the job/income plan and truthful required reporting; update the calendar when pay or benefits are actually received.

## Keep the evidence private and useful

For each interaction, keep the date, agency/provider, contact route, question, factual answer, reference number, next action, and deadline. Save the balance record or screenshot, final certifications, determinations, benefit applications, hardship requests, and submission/payment confirmations alongside it. Keep received money and unresolved requests easy to reconcile. Store completed worksheets, claim identifiers, bank details, medical information, and screenshots privately, outside public issues and repositories.
