# Reproductive and Maternal Care Curation

This is the bounded U.S. first edition for [issue #139](https://github.com/egohygiene/akashic/issues/139), under [roadmap #40](https://github.com/egohygiene/akashic/issues/40). It extends [Health and Well-Being](../lists/health-and-well-being/README.md) and reuses the appointment, referral, records, accessibility, and emergency-navigation foundation delivered in #138.

## Canonical home and smallest useful edition

Ten health resources support contraception and sexual-health services, fertility education, reproductive-care discovery, prenatal and postpartum care, maternal warning information, emotional support, and pregnancy/infant-loss support. One additional resource, Repro Legal Helpline, belongs in Legal Help and Law. Three new health topics and a focused section inside the existing portal guide make the paths independently usable without another collection or application.

The guide labels education, professional-society material, advocacy and provider discovery, legal assistance, emotional support, and peer support by their actual roles. It includes practical questions about clinical services, provider qualifications, costs, consent, privacy, accessible appointments, and language assistance. It distinguishes routine scheduling, urgent clinical warning information, and emergency response without supplying a diagnostic or treatment workflow.

## Metadata, exclusions, and safety boundaries

Every addition has an explicit stable ID and controlled resource type, role, authority, access, geography, language, platform, account, license, status, sensitivity, volatility, and review cadence. `linkStatus: "ok"` and `linkChecked: "2026-09-17"` record public-page availability; no `reviewed` field is added. Public reading is account-free. Participation, clinic care, and legal intake can require additional information or arrangements even when the landing page does not require an account.

`access: ["free"]` describes the public reference or specified support service. It does not promise free consultations, tests, contraception, fertility treatment, procedures, or all linked offerings. Medical sensitivity applies throughout, with legal, privacy, youth, emergency, and crisis tags where relevant. Directories, the HRSA hotline, and Repro Legal Helpline use monthly review/high volatility; general education and grief-support resources use quarterly review/medium volatility. The first edition's U.S. geography is explicit; international reach of some existing or supporting organizations is not treated as identical service availability everywhere.

An ACOG birth-control page returned a fetch error, and a proposed Planned Parenthood pregnancy-options article returned forbidden access. They were omitted rather than marked verified; accessible federal and professional-society sources supply the selected education. An attempted MedlinePlus pregnancy-loss URL did not resolve; NICHD supplies the correctly scoped primary overview. Additional generic birth-control sites, fertility clinics, local providers, paid apps, symptom tools, and clinical regimens were excluded to keep this edition bounded. Broader parenting, caregiver, and bereavement administration work remains in its own roadmap slices, and prior-authorization reimbursement remains #118.

The guide preserves the existing 911/988 distinction and directs urgent maternal concerns to clinical care. It does not treat peer support, pregnancy counseling, advertising, advocacy, a directory listing, or legal intake as proof of clinical services. It asks readers to verify the actual services and clinician qualifications and to keep private health information out of public issue reports.
