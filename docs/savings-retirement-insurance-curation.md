# Savings, retirement, and insurance curation

Issue: [#137](https://github.com/egohygiene/akashic/issues/137), under [#40](https://github.com/egohygiene/akashic/issues/40). Extends the [everyday-money edition](everyday-money-curation.md) in [Personal Finance and Insurance](../lists/personal-finance-and-insurance/README.md).

## Scope and canonical homes

Add short paths for emergency reserves, investment risk and fees, verifying financial help, retirement plans and old accounts, and insurance decisions. The marked Markdown guide publishes the decision table and comparison checklist through the existing portal renderer. Detailed resource sections remain independently useful on GitHub. No stock picks, allocation targets, projected returns, provider rankings, personal tax planning, or new local-provider directory are introduced.

Long-term-care material includes 2018 cost examples; the entry visibly distinguishes historical figures from current quotes. No cost amount or promise of coverage is reproduced. |

Disability-income discovery starts with existing employer benefits and the canonical state-regulator directory, then asks about the policy's definition, waiting period, benefit duration, offsets, and portability. It does not substitute Social Security disability benefits or long-term care for private income protection. The narrow income-replacement distinction is supported by NAIC's [2016 caregiver article](https://content.naic.org/article/consumer-insight-insurance-considerations-caregivers). That article is excluded as a catalog recommendation because it also contains dated health-insurance penalty guidance. Unverified guessed disability-guide URLs are not published.

NAIC's [general insurance explainer](https://content.naic.org/consumer/how-does-insurance-work) contains unfilled department-name and hyperlink placeholders; it is excluded. Commercial quote funnels, affiliate comparisons, insurer rankings, and another adviser/provider directory add no necessary value to this edition and are omitted.

Every new or moved resource has an explicit stable ID and full applicable access, authority, geography, language, platform, account, license, status, volatility, review cadence, sensitivity, and link metadata. NAIC and FINRA use `nonprofit` authority; federal agency guides use `official`. All new resources are free public information or discovery. Unknown licenses remain unknown; no `reviewed` date is asserted. The guide asks readers to verify current tax-year and policy/plan rules without publishing numerical limits, tax examples, or fixed transaction deadlines.
