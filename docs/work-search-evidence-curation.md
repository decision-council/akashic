## Scope and Ownership

The canonical entry point is [Work and Learning — Prepare, apply, and follow up](../lists/work-and-learning/README.md#prepare-apply-and-follow-up). The smallest useful edition is a private activity record, an agency-aware weekly worksheet, a separate company/role tracker, mapping rules, and a short reconciliation routine. It is a documentation workflow, not an automated certification tool or a new catalog/schema subsystem.

## Editorial Boundaries

- Event IDs, application IDs, evidence paths, rule checks, and mappings are editorial organization aids. No public JSON Schema, software integration, or agency endorsement is implied.
- A current application status is not proof of a new activity. The guide separates the date an action occurred from when it was recorded and keeps submitted snapshots independent of later pipeline changes.
- Scouting, culling, résumé/profile/portfolio work, interview preparation, networking, and counseling are recorded truthfully; qualification stays unresolved until matched to the applicable agency rule or individual plan. An observed posting count is not an automatic activity count.
- The small weekly routine does not reduce required activities, excuse omitted disclosures, or justify a false certification. No automatic applications, generated quota entries, or external messages are created.
- Preserve required agency evidence without collecting unrelated third-party details. Completed records, private correspondence, identifiers, and claim screenshots stay outside public repositories and derivatives.
- Counts stay **5,355 resources, 36 collections, 479 topics, and 147 Atlas resources**; Work and Learning stays **194 resources**. Existing resource entries, IDs, topics, metadata, and budgets remain unchanged.

## Medium Article Candidate

**Title:** “Your Job Search Log and Your Application Tracker Are Not the Same Thing.”

**Reader promise:** Keep an accurate weekly record while retaining a useful view of applications that unfold over many weeks.

**Outline:**

1. Start with a clearly fictional application submitted one week and followed up the next. The pending application is not a fresh submission every week.
2. Introduce three views: what happened, the agency's weekly record, and the current company/role pipeline.
3. Show how a scouting session, a later application, and a counselor appointment need different descriptions and evidence.
4. Explain agency verification before mapping activities, including official formats, repeat-activity restrictions, and required fields.
5. Use the five-step routine: capture, log, reconcile, submit when required, preserve.
6. End with the blank worksheet, synthetic example, privacy boundary, and official-source links.

Avoid claims that the template guarantees compliance, a particular activity always counts, or a certain quota applies nationwide. Do not adapt private application histories into examples. Link the [maintained guide](work-search-evidence.md) and recheck the sources before publication.
