## Start Here

You do not need to work through this whole page. Pick one next action.

**1. Need a person to help find a starting point?** Call 211 or use the [211 and Findhelp entries](../public-services-and-support/README.md#benefits-and-emergency-assistance). Ask for the program serving your ZIP code and the need that cannot wait.

**2. Choose the immediate problem:** [food](#food-and-nutrition), [rent](#housing-and-rent), [utilities](#utilities-and-communications), [medical bills](#health-coverage-and-medical-bills), or [prescriptions](#prescriptions). With an eviction, court, repossession, or shutoff notice, also seek [legal help](#taxes-and-legal-aid); an aid application does not automatically pause a deadline.

**3. If you have capacity for one more step:** check [benefits](#benefits-screening) after an income change, or contact the existing provider's hardship team through the number on your bill. Ask what help is available, what evidence they need, when they will decide, and what happens while the request is pending.

### Types of help

| Label             | What it means                                                              | Check before relying on it                                                     |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Direct assistance | A program may provide money, food, coverage, or pay an eligible expense.   | Eligibility, funding, approval, payment timing, and renewal.                   |
| Payment relief    | An existing creditor or provider may change the payment schedule or terms. | Interest, fees, total cost, credit reporting, duration, and written approval.  |
| Discount          | The price may fall; an amount may still be due.                            | Actual price, participating provider, insurance interaction, and expiration.   |
| Navigation        | A directory, screener, or adviser connects you with another organization.  | Who actually administers the help; a referral is not an award.                 |
| Information only  | A guide explains options and where to ask.                                 | The current agency or provider rules; the guide cannot approve an application. |

Entries can combine labels. **Free information or a free application does not mean every downstream service is free.**

### Keep the next step small and private

Keep a private note with the program, contact, notice deadline, next action, and status: **identified → contacted → applied → awaiting decision → approved or declined**. Record a renewal date only when the provider gives one. Pending help is not available cash.

Ask for the program's document checklist before collecting everything. Depending on the program, it may request proof of identity, residence, household income, a lease or bill, insurance details, employment history, or medical evidence. Ask what alternatives are accepted if something is missing. Keep application receipts and written decisions; use the notice's review or appeal route if needed.

### Scam and privacy checks

Use the agency's own application route or an independently verified provider. Reject guaranteed grants or debt erasure, gift-card or cryptocurrency demands, and paid access to government applications. Review fees before agreeing to counseling or debt management. Do not change payment arrangements based solely on a sales pitch.

Share only what the verified program requires through its designated channel. Do not post Social Security numbers, bank details, diagnoses, benefit notices, or financial documents in public issues, forms, or mutual-aid posts. Ask directories how referrals share your information. Prescription discount cards are not insurance; compare the actual pharmacy price and plan treatment before using one.

Curators follow the [monthly review and metadata policy](../../docs/financial-relief-curation.md); automated link checks cannot establish eligibility or continued funding.

## Local and Disaster Assistance

For declared disasters, check the existing [DisasterAssistance.gov and FEMA recovery-center routes](../awesome-abundance/public-programs-and-services/README.md#disaster-and-safety-support).
